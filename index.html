    How to build

    • The toolbar uses icons: hold the pointer over one to see its name. In order: Build, Erase, Select, Box, Pick, Sign, Person, Door, Copy, Paste, Mirror, Symmetry, Undo, Redo, Clear, Day, Sunset, Night, Account, Gallery, Reports (admins, or anyone picked to review one; a red number shows how many are waiting), New, Save, Picture, Help.
    • Look around: drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag (or two fingers) to move.
    • Place a block: pick one below, then click the ground or another block. You can also press P to place the selected block at the pointer.
    • Start something new: the New icon (page with a plus) offers starting scenes: a grass field, a grass field with a tree, a forest, a small island or an empty stand. Pick Small, Medium or Large, or choose Custom to type your own width and depth in blocks (12 to 96). Tick Keep it square to use one number for both.
    • Remove a block: right-click it, or hold Shift and click. (The Erase tool does the same.)
    • Build fast: Box fills a whole area — click one corner, then the opposite corner. Hold Shift on the second click to clear a box.
    • Copy a block: Pick takes the block you click on.
    • Copy and paste: Select — click one corner, then the opposite corner; it is copied automatically. Then Paste: a green footprint follows your pointer — move it where you want, click to place it, Rotate (or Q) to turn it and the flip button to mirror it left-to-right. Click Done or press Esc when finished. With an area selected, press Delete to remove everything in it.
    • Mirror: the Mirror tool (or M) asks for an area — click one corner, then the opposite corner — and then for the mirror line: move the pointer beside the area and a blue line shows where it will go, with a preview of the flipped copy. Click to make the copy. Signs are mirrored too; people and doors are not. Esc stops.
    • Build symmetrically: click the Symmetry icon (or press Y) to cycle off, left-right, front-back and both. Everything you place, remove or fill is repeated on the other side of the blue line.
    • Fly (point of view): only on devices with a keyboard. The eye button (or F) lets you fly around: up/down arrows go forward and back, left/right arrows turn, Shift + left/right slide sideways, Shift + up/down rise and sink, dragging looks around. Building works the same while you fly. Esc or F stops.
    • Doors: the Door tool (or D) puts a door, one block wide and two tall like Minecraft's, where you click. Select any planks colour first to colour it; click a door to open or close it, with any tool except Erase, Box and Select (with the Door tool and a different planks colour selected, a click repaints it instead), press R over it to turn it a quarter turn or Shift+R to flip it to the other side of its block, and use Erase to remove it.
    • Animate: the film button (or A) opens the Animate bar. Choose Walk, click a person, then click where they should walk to; choose Turn, click a person, then click the spot they should turn to face (they spin on the spot); or choose Door and click a door to open or close it. Steps play one after another (tick the box to start a step at the same time as the one before). Press Play to watch, Start as a person to see it through someone's eyes (you walk and turn with their steps; with none, the arrow keys move you), or Record video to save the playback as a video file: people turn, walk with swinging arms and legs, and doors swing. Stop, or Done, puts everything back where it was. Steps are saved with the diorama. People walk in a straight line and do not go around blocks.
    • People: the Person tool (or H) puts a figure where you click and opens a window to change their hair, hat, shirt, pants, shoes and name. Click a person in any tool to change them later (Move picks them up and puts them somewhere else; Look through their eyes: arrows walk, Shift + left/right spins them around, Shift + up lies them face down or gets them up, Shift + down lies them on their back or gets them up, dragging turns the head, Head straight matches the head to the body, Esc finishes). Right-click removes one.
    • Classes: in the Gallery you can join a class with the code from your teacher, or start your own class and give out its code. When you Save, choose "In a class gallery" to share with just that class.
    • Name things: Sign puts floating text in your scene.
    • Mistake? Cmd/Ctrl+Z undoes it.
    • Share it: Picture saves a PNG you can attach to a post. To share a link, open My dioramas and press Copy link on one of yours; it opens the saved one with no sign-in needed (and viewers can report it). Only dioramas in the everyone gallery without a code have a link.

    Keys: 1–9 pick blocks · B build · P places a block at the pointer · E erase · X box · S select · Delete clears the selection · C copy · V paste · M mirror · Y symmetry · I pick · T sign. Your diorama saves itself in this browser.
